DOE for Hyperparameter Optimization of Machine Learning Models
Idea: Use factorial or response-surface designs to study ML hyperparameters systematically.

For example, for Random Forest:

number of trees
maximum depth
minimum samples per leaf
number of candidate features
Use a 2-level factorial design for screening, followed by RSM for optimization.

Research question:

Can DOE achieve comparable or better hyperparameter selection than conventional grid/random search with substantially fewer ML model evaluations?

This is probably one of the most straightforward DOE + ML papers.


A very natural final project would be:

Design of Experiments and Machine Learning for Prediction and Optimization\boxed{\textbf{Design of Experiments and Machine Learning for Prediction and Optimization}}
where students design an experiment using a factorial/response-surface design, fit both a classical response-surface model and an ML model, compare predictive performance, and then use SHAP or another XAI method to interpret the ML model.
