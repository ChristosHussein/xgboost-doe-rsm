## Remaining limitations

The historical holdout was exposed before revised isolation. Dataset-sampling uncertainty and new sentiment-data generalization are not established. The extension uses fixed-block OLS inference, not unseen-block Satterthwaite inference. GP intervals and attribution agreement need empirical confirmation. Archived milestone reports are historical supporting records, not a substitute for the claim-bound manuscript. A green software workflow is not scientific validation of the new method.

## Cleanup and preservation

Frozen canonical evidence, statistical tests, publication sources and empirical figures remain available. Disposable build products and preview caches are excluded from the publication package.
